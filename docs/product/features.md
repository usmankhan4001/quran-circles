# Features that make this ours

Beyond the basics, these features connect people to a local session and help coordinators run it.

## Attendee side

- **Session companion (WP17):** coordinators set the portion for each session (a Para, a Surah or ayah range, or a lesson title). The portion links into the Quran reader, with a read-ahead link before the session. After the session the coordinator can add a short recap note in Urdu or English. Attendees see both under My Journey.
- **Request a circle near me (WP19, after launch):** a signed-in person picks their area and optionally days, section and language. Only the area is kept as location. One active request per person per area; requests expire and can be cancelled. Admins see demand by city and area. When requests in an area pass an admin-set threshold, the city coordinator is alerted. When a circle opens there, requesters hear by push first, and by WhatsApp only if they consented.
- **Venue facilities (WP17):** venue coordinators fill a fixed checklist: women's section, parking, wudu area, access for elders and wheelchairs, child-friendly, entrance notes. It shows on every venue and session.
- **Invite friends and family (WP17):** share a session as a share card, made by the same renderer as posters, with a deep link that opens straight to registration. No referral rewards.

## Staff side

- **Session posters (WP16):** admins and coordinators (within scope) generate branded posters from curated templates with editable fields (text, colours, language). Everything else is locked. Formats: story or status size (9:16), print-ready PDF (A4 or A3), and the link preview image. Bilingual with a QR code. If the session's time or venue changes afterwards, the poster shows an out-of-date warning and can be regenerated. Not in V2: speaker photos, weekly multi-session posters, a drag-and-drop editor, a square image.
- **Message templates (WP15):** admins keep approved Urdu and English wording. Coordinators pick one, fill the blanks and post within their scope. WhatsApp sends use the matching approved template.
- **Demand map and alerts (WP19):** requests by area, venues with no coordinator, sessions with low sign-ups. Thresholds are settings.
- **Coordinator handover (WP15):** reassign a venue or circle; history stays; the previous coordinator loses access at once; audited.
- **Content manager (WP15):** About, FAQ and help pages edited in the console in English and Urdu, with draft and publish.
- **Admin reports (WP15):** registrations by city, venue and day; active users and opens; notification delivery and opens; attendance trends; sign-up funnel; coordinator response time; message spend; data quality; Dora to Circles conversion; CSV exports; audit log.

## Admin app, top tasks on a phone

Post an update or change a session time. Create or edit a venue or circle, with city approval where needed. See and contact today's registrants. Mark attendance on a quick tick list.

## Home-screen widget (WP20, after launch)

Shows the next session first and the next prayer second. Needs native code per platform.
