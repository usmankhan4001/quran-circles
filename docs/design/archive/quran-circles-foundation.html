Step 1 of the redesign · for your approval

The foundation every new screen is built from

Colour, type, spacing and the core parts, for the member app, the coordinator app and the console. Nothing here is in the code yet. Once you agree it, I draw the screens journey by journey, in English and Urdu, and you approve each one before it is built.

Starting point

What carries over, and what this fixes

Kept from earlier decisions

  • Join pledge on the session page, no seat booking.
  • Urdu designed first, right to left, never squeezed to fit.
  • Four text sizes, 48 px touch targets, AA contrast, nothing told by colour alone.
  • Loading, empty, error and offline drawn for every list.
  • One main action per screen. Green brand from your logo. Mehr Nastaliq for Urdu, King Fahd Hafs for Quran text, Plus Jakarta Sans for English.

Problems it is built to fix

  • Urdu readability: Urdu body text goes from about 13 px to 20 px with room between lines.
  • Two action colours: green is the only action colour; the charcoal buttons go.
  • Clip art and white-on-photo text: replaced by one quiet eight-point star, used sparingly.
  • Icon-only tabs: every tab has a word under its icon.
  • Map, session screens, coordinator app, console, poster maker: redrawn from this kit in step 2.

2

Type, with Urdu sized for reading

Urdu gets its own scale instead of the English sizes. Mehr Nastaliq reads small for its point size and needs about twice the line height, so every Urdu role is two to four points bigger than its English twin. Urdu has one weight; emphasis comes from size and colour, never fake bold. Numbers, times and phone numbers stay in Western digits, left to right, inside Urdu lines.

Three choices before I draw screens

1. Which green?

This page uses the green from your logo, #0A6938. The current app uses a darker emerald (#1E563B) and earlier drafts used the poster green (#226836). They are close; the logo green is brighter and passes contrast on white.

I suggest the logo green, so app, icon and posters match.

2. Urdu size

Urdu body is 20 px here, English is 16. Read the comparison above and the Urdu column on your phone. Is it comfortable, too big, or still small?

I suggest 20 px; it is the biggest single fix for most of our users.

3. Where should the redesign start?

Member app (Home, Nearby with the map, session page and join), coordinator app (Today, attendance, posters), or the console (sessions, the new session page, poster maker).

I suggest the member app's find-and-join journey first, because most people see it and the map and session screens were on your list.
