# Coordinator guide

For venue coordinators and city coordinators. You run your venue or circle from the Quran Circles coordinator app on your phone. Reports, bulk import and exports are in the [console](console-guide.md) on a computer.

## Your role and your place

You work in one place: a venue (venue coordinator) or a city with all its venues (city coordinator). The top of the Today screen says it, for example "You are working as Venue coordinator, Sample Masjid Al-Noor". You see and change only what is in your place. If something is outside it, the app says it was not found.

- You are invited by an admin or a city coordinator by phone number. You get a WhatsApp invitation, accept it and sign in with a code. There is no password.
- Everything you do is recorded: who, what, where and when.
- If you hand over a venue, the next coordinator gets access and you lose it immediately. History stays.

## The four tabs

| Tab     | Use it to                                                          |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Today   | See today's sessions and the next ones, with quick actions on each |
| Updates | Post a message to your people and see what you posted              |
| Places  | Edit your venue and its circles                                    |
| Account | Language, text size, theme, sign out                               |

On a session card the quick actions are **Registrants**, **Attendance**, **Post update** and **Change time**.

## See who joined

Open **Registrants** on a session. You see the names and phone numbers of the people who joined, so you can contact them. Treat the list as private.

- Joining is a pledge, not a reserved seat. There are no seat counts to manage.
- If an older session still has a limit set, people over it are shown as waiting for confirmation.
- Do not copy the list into group chats or share screenshots. To export a file you need the console, and the file carries a reminder that it holds personal details.

## Mark attendance

Open **Attendance** on a session after it takes place.

1. Enter the headcount.
2. Add names for the people you know, if you wish. Names are optional.
3. Save. You can change it later.

Attendance is a coordinator task only. Members do not check in.

## Post an update

Use **Updates** or **Post update** on a session. An update reaches people connected to your venue (or your city, if you are a city coordinator).

1. Pick a message template. Approved wording in English and Urdu is kept by the admins.
2. Fill in the blanks (the time, the topic).
3. Look at **how many people it will reach**. Nothing is sent yet.
4. Send. The update appears in members' Updates feed, and goes out as a notification. WhatsApp is used only for important changes.

Keep updates short and useful. A message cannot be unsent; check the preview first.

## Change a session time or cancel a date

1. Open the session and choose **Change time**.
2. Pick the new time or day, and from which date it applies.
3. Read the summary and confirm. The app asks you to confirm because people will be told.
4. Reminders move to the new time by themselves, and an update can be posted for you.

To cancel one date, open that date and choose **Cancel**. People who joined are told. Once a session is cancelled, its time cannot be changed.

## Set the day's portion and a recap

For each date you can set the **portion**: a Para, a surah and ayah range, or a lesson title. Members see it, with a link into the Quran reader. After the session you can add a short **recap** in Urdu or English.

## Make a poster

Posters are made from approved templates.

1. Open a session and choose **Poster**.
2. Pick a template, a language (English, Urdu or both) and the formats you need: story size, A4, A3 or a link preview picture.
3. Type the title and details. The date, time and venue come from the session, so the poster can never disagree with it.
4. Wait a moment. The poster appears as ready; download the PNG or PDF.

If the session's time or venue changes later, the poster shows **out of date**. Choose **Regenerate** to make a fresh one; the old one is replaced when the new one is ready. You can make a limited number of posters a minute.

## Your venue and circles

In **Places** you can:

- edit your venue's details, its public contact number and its facilities (women's section, parking, wudu area, access for elders and wheelchairs, child-friendly, entrance notes);
- create a circle or session. A new circle created by a venue coordinator waits for the city coordinator's approval before it goes public;
- edit a circle: the teacher's name (optional), the language, the section and the schedule.

City coordinators also approve or reject circles waiting in their city, create and archive venues, and invite venue coordinators.

## Good habits

- Mark attendance on the day while it is fresh.
- Post an update for any change, even a small one.
- Keep phone numbers private. Do not share the registrant list.
- Sign out on a shared phone.
- If you think someone else has your login, tell your city coordinator at once. They can revoke it immediately.

## Accessibility

The coordinator app has the same four text sizes, dark mode and high contrast as the member app, works right to left in Urdu, and has controls of at least 48 dp. If a screen is hard to use, tell your city coordinator so it can be fixed.
