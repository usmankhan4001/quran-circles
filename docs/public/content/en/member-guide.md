# Member guide

Quran Circles helps you find the nearest Quran session, join it in a tap and never miss it. You do not need an account to look around. You need a WhatsApp number to join.

## Get the app

- **Android:** install Quran Circles from Google Play (an internal test is running first).
- **Any phone or computer:** open `https://qurancircle.doraquran.pk` in your browser. On a phone choose "Add to Home Screen" and it opens like an app. On iPhone this is also how you get notifications.

## The five places in the app

| Place                           | What is there                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home                            | Your next circle first, then circles near you, the ayah of the day and shortcuts to the Quran and prayer times. In Ramadan it shows tonight's Dora. |
| Nearby                          | A map and a list of venues and circles. Search by area or place name; filter by day, time, section (women, men, family), language and area.         |
| Quran                           | Surahs and juz, bookmarks, your last place and an offline copy. Prayer times with a countdown and the Hijri date are one tap away.                  |
| My Journey                      | Your joined sessions with a reminder switch each, the progress of your circles and the places you follow.                                           |
| Profile (the circle at the top) | Language, text size, dark mode, alerts for each place, your account and help.                                                                       |

The four tabs are Home, Nearby, Quran and My Journey. Every tab keeps its name for screen readers.

## Find a session

1. Open **Nearby**.
2. Choose your area, or type a place name in the search box.
3. Narrow the list with the day, time, section and language filters.
4. Switch between **List** and **Map**.
5. Tap a session to see the venue, the time, how long it runs, the section and any facilities such as a women's section, parking, a wudu area and access for elders and wheelchairs.

If nothing is close, the list ends with **Ask for a circle near you**. Choose your area (and, if you like, days, section and language). Only your area is kept. You can have one active request per area and cancel it any time. When a circle opens there you hear first.

## Join a session

Joining is a **pledge to come**. No seat is reserved and you are never turned away at the door because of a count.

1. Open the session and tap **Join** at the bottom.
2. A sheet asks for your name, your WhatsApp number, your area and the section you will sit in (women, men or family).
3. If you are not signed in, a six-digit code arrives on WhatsApp. Type it in. It lasts five minutes.
4. You get a confirmation. Your session now appears in **My Journey**.

You will be reminded before each session, and told if the time changes or a session is cancelled.

> **Note.** Only Pakistani mobile numbers (starting `+92 3`) can receive a code. If you do not get a code, check that the number is on WhatsApp, wait a minute and ask again. After too many tries you are asked to wait; the screen says how long.

## Change your mind

Open **My Journey**, choose the session and tap **Cancel**. The app asks you to confirm, because this is the one step you cannot take back with a single tap (you can always join again).

## Reminders and messages

The app sends reminders from your phone first, because that costs nothing. WhatsApp is used for your sign-in code, your confirmation, important changes and one reminder per session if your phone cannot receive notifications.

- Turn reminders on or off for each session in **My Journey**.
- Turn alerts on or off for each place you follow in **Profile**.
- **One tap stops WhatsApp messages** from Quran Circles. You can also reply STOP to any message.
- The **Updates** feed in the app keeps every announcement, with a count of unread ones. National updates reach everyone; city and venue updates reach people connected to them.

## Read the Quran and check prayer times

- Choose a surah or a juz. The Urdu translation is shown under each verse.
- Add a **bookmark**; the app also remembers your last place.
- Save passages for offline reading. The app keeps up to 24 recent passages on your device.
- **Prayer times** are calculated on your phone for where you are, with a countdown to the next prayer and today's Hijri date. The Hijri date can be a day different from your local moon sighting; the national admin adjusts it.
- **I read today** records a day you read, so the app can say "4 of 7 days this week". There are no streaks and no pressure.

## Look after your account

In **Profile** you can change your name and language, set the text size and theme, choose alerts and read the help pages.

**Delete my account** removes your registrations and personal details. It cannot be undone. Only anonymous totals remain, such as how many people read on a day.

## Make it easier to read

- **Four text sizes** apply to the whole app. The largest step is for people who want very large text.
- **Dark mode** and **high contrast** are in Profile.
- **Urdu** mirrors the whole app right to left. Numbers stay as 0 to 9, and phone numbers and codes stay left to right.
- Everything works with a screen reader (TalkBack or VoiceOver) and with a keyboard on the web.

## If something is wrong

| Problem                         | What to try                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No code arrived                 | Check the number starts `+92 3`, that WhatsApp is installed on it, and wait for the "send again" time. Do not ask repeatedly. |
| "Too many tries"                | Wait the time shown, then ask for a new code.                                                                                 |
| The session time changed        | Open **Updates**; the change is posted there. Your reminder moves by itself.                                                  |
| I cannot find a circle          | Widen the area, clear a filter, or use **Ask for a circle near you**.                                                         |
| Text looks too small or cramped | Change the text size in Profile.                                                                                              |
| Urdu looks odd                  | Update the app; the Urdu text is being reviewed. Tell the coordinator if a word is wrong.                                     |
| I want to leave                 | Cancel your sessions, then **Delete my account** in Profile.                                                                  |

For anything else, contact the coordinator of your venue or write to the support address shown in the app's Help.
